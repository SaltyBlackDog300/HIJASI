PRICING

Free to start. A flat monthly plan when you grow.

Start on Starter with no subscription and a small fee per top-up, or switch to a monthly plan with no top-up fees once you have more customers.

Payment-processing fees are separate. The payment provider (card, UPI or local gateway) charges its own processing fee, which the business covers. Kassara's fees are on top of that and never come out of the customer's wallet balance.

Starter top-up fee: the customer pays the top-up plus the Kassara fee, e.g. a €50 top-up costs €51.50 and adds €50 to the wallet. The full breakdown is shown before checkout.

Proposed launch prices. Monthly top-up volume allowances for Business and Business Pro, and the payment and regulatory arrangements for real money, are not final yet. Kassara currently runs in test mode only.
