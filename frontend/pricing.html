Pricing

Free to start.
Grow into a plan.

Start on Starter with no subscription and a small fee per top-up, or switch to a flat monthly plan with no top-up fees once your customers keep coming back.

Processing fees are separate.

The payment provider (card, UPI or local gateway) charges its own processing fee, which the business covers. Kassara's fees never come out of the customer's wallet balance.

Shown before checkout.

On Starter the customer pays the top-up plus the Kassara fee. A €50 top-up costs €51.50 and adds €50 to the wallet, and the full breakdown is shown before they pay.

Proposed launch prices.

Monthly top-up volume allowances for Business and Business Pro, and the payment and regulatory arrangements for real money, are not final yet. Kassara runs in test mode only.
