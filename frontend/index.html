HOW IT WORKS

From application to first wallet payment.

1

Apply

Businesses declare who they are and what they sell. Every application is reviewed by hand. No crypto, gambling or money transfer.

2

Integrate

Approved merchants get a secret key and call our REST API from their own server.

3

Top up

Customers pay through the merchant's gateway. The wallet is credited only after the gateway confirms.

4

Spend

Orders are paid from the balance, with an append-only ledger, refunds, limits and idempotency.

◈
CLOSED-LOOP BY DESIGN

Each wallet only works where it was issued.

No cash-out and no transfers between merchants. This matches the closed-system prepaid instruments that India's RBI does not require authorisation for. We run the software and ledger; the merchant keeps the money.

⌁

Built for acquisition

Payment providers in emerging markets can white-label Kassara to offer stored-value wallets on top of the card and UPI rails they already run, opening new use cases for every merchant they serve.

PRICING

Free to start. A flat monthly plan when you grow.

Payment-processing fees are separate. The payment provider (card, UPI or local gateway) charges its own processing fee, which the business covers. Kassara's fees are on top of that and never come out of the customer's wallet balance.

Starter top-up fee: the customer pays the top-up plus the Kassara fee, e.g. a €50 top-up costs €51.50 and adds €50 to the wallet. The full breakdown is shown before checkout.

Proposed launch prices. Monthly top-up volume allowances for Business and Business Pro, and the payment and regulatory arrangements for real money, are not final yet. Kassara currently runs in test mode only.
