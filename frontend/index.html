How it works

1. Apply

Businesses register. We review every merchant by hand — no crypto, no gambling, no money transfer.

2. Integrate

Approved merchants get a secret key and call our REST API from their server.

3. Top up

Customers pay through the merchant's own gateway. We credit the wallet only after the gateway confirms.

4. Spend

Orders are paid from the wallet balance, with a full audit ledger, refunds and limits.

Closed-loop by design

Each wallet only works at the business that issued it.

No cash-out, no transfers between merchants. This mirrors the "closed system" prepaid instruments that India's RBI does not require authorisation for — the same model BigBasket's wallet uses. We provide the software and ledger; the merchant keeps the money in their own account.

Built for acquisition

A drop-in wallet layer for any gateway.

Payment providers in emerging markets can white-label HIJASI to offer wallets on top of the card and UPI rails they already run — instantly unlocking stored-value use cases for their merchants.
