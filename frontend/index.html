HOW IT WORKS

From application to first wallet payment.

1

Apply

Businesses declare who they are and what they sell. Every application is reviewed by hand. No crypto, gambling or money transfer.

2

Integrate

Approved merchants get a secret key and call our REST API from their own server.

3

Top up

Customers pay through the merchant's gateway. The wallet is credited only after the gateway confirms.

4

Spend

Orders are paid from the balance, with an append-only ledger, refunds, limits and idempotency.

◈
CLOSED-LOOP BY DESIGN

Each wallet only works where it was issued.

No cash-out and no transfers between merchants. This matches the closed-system prepaid instruments that India's RBI does not require authorisation for. We run the software and ledger; the merchant keeps the money.

⌁

Built for acquisition

Payment providers in emerging markets can white-label HIJASI to offer stored-value wallets on top of the card and UPI rails they already run, opening new use cases for every merchant they serve.
